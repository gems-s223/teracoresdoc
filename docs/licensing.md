# Account & Licensing

- Signing in and license expiry are covered in [Getting Started](getting-started.md) — your administrator issues credentials and manages renewals.
- **About → About Teracores** shows version/app information.
- **About → Documentation** opens documentation from within the app.
- **About → Check for Updates** checks whether a newer version is available.

If your access is revoked or your trial period ends, you'll see an **Access ended** dialog explaining when it ended and asking you to contact your Teracores administrator to renew — this can happen on any launch, not just the first one.
