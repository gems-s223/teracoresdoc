# Settings & Preferences

**File → Settings** opens the app's preferences dialog, organized into tabs:

- **Lines** — per-category line color, width, dash style, and opacity for wells, casing, perforations, feedzones, wiremesh, and similar drawn elements.
- **Labels** — well/cluster label color, size, and short-name options.
- **Chart** — legend position for charts.
- **Project** — project-level settings: field name, UTM zone, datum, and well-code convention. (Of the datum choices offered, only **WGS84** currently drives the basemap's coordinate math — the others are available for labeling/parity but aren't fully wired up yet.)
- **Theme** — switch between **Light** and **Dark** app themes. You can also toggle theme directly with **Ctrl+D** (dark) / **Ctrl+L** (light) from anywhere in the app.

Most of these settings are per-Windows-user (they follow you between projects); the Project tab's fields are the exception — those are saved into the project itself, since a UTM zone belongs to the project, not to your user account.
